How It Works

Four simple steps to launch your AI infrastructure

1

Installation

We install the 10×10×6 unit in your home office, garage, or basement. Requires 240V, 50A circuit. Full environmental monitoring included.

Configuration

Our team remotely configures your environment, loads base models (Llama, Mistral, Stable Diffusion), and optimizes for your workload.

3

Integration

4

Optimize & Scale

Monitor via web dashboard. Stack additional units for training. We handle firmware updates and model library expansion.
