# HomeAI DataCenter - Executive Summary

## The Opportunity

Enterprises spend $50B+ annually on cloud AI services with skyrocketing costs and no end in sight. We offer an alternative: enterprise-grade AI infrastructure you own and control, deployed in 4-6 weeks.

**The Math:** A company spending $50K/month on AWS AI services ($600K/year) recovers their $420K hardware investment in 8-10 months, then eliminates cloud costs entirely.

---

## The Solution

A modular, turnkey AI data center system designed for non-technical teams to deploy and manage.

**What You Get:**
- 8× NVIDIA A100 GPUs (640 TFLOPS) or up to 16× H100s
- Pre-loaded with industry models: Llama, Mistral, Stable Diffusion, etc.
- OpenAI-compatible REST API (works with existing code)
- Web dashboard for monitoring and management
- Professional installation and 24/7 support
- Zero ongoing software costs

**Form Factor:** Fits in a garage, office, or basement (10×10×6 ft, 3,500 lbs)
