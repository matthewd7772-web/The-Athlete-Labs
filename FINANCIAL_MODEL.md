# HomeAI DataCenter - Detailed Financial Model

## Revenue Model & Assumptions

### Key Assumptions

**Hardware Sales**
- Year 1: 15 units
- Year 2: 45 units (+200% growth)
- Year 3: 120 units (+167% growth)
- Year 4: 250 units (+108% growth)
- Year 5: 400 units (+60% growth)

**Pricing by Tier**
- Starter: $280K (25% of sales)
- Professional: $420K (65% of sales) ← Blended average
- Enterprise: Custom (10% of sales)
- Blended ASP: $362K-$338K (declining as market matures)

**Hardware Gross Margin**
- Year 1-2: 55% (established pricing, volume discounts not yet realized)
- Year 3-5: 52-48% (scale discounts to COGS, pricing compression)

**Services Revenue** (Installation + Customization)
- Installation fee: $15K-$35K per unit (avg $25K in Year 1)
- Attachment rate: 100% of units
- Growth follows hardware units

**Support Plans** (Recurring Revenue)
- Year 2+: 85-94% attach rate of installed base
- Basic: 2% of hardware cost annually ($8.4K for $420K unit)
- Professional: 7% of hardware cost ($29.4K)
- Enterprise: 12% of hardware cost ($50.4K+)
- Blended average: 6-7% by Year 3
- Gross margin: 75-80% (mostly support staff, some cloud resources)

**Professional Services** (Consulting, Custom Deployments)
- Year 2: $500K (project-based)
- Year 3+: 4-5% of total revenue
- Gross margin: 65-70%
